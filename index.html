About the platform

Your Library, Connected.

CDM LibHub is designed to bring campus library resources, circulation records, and student services into an organized and accessible digital experience. It provides a central platform where students and faculty can find references, monitor due dates, and borrow without long lines or manual paper logs.

Our objectives

  • Make academic textbooks and syllabi references easier to discover
  • Eliminate physical logbooks with digital QR entrance attendance
  • Streamline borrowing counter checkouts, returns, and reservation staging
  • Ensure zero monetary fine burden with transparent SMS loan reminders
  • Empower librarians with accurate collection telemetry and audit reports

Installation guide

Take LibHub With You

LibHub is used on your phone — install the app to your home screen to get started. Librarians use the administrative interface on a desktop or laptop.

Install on Android

  1. 1Open the official CDM LibHub application link using Google Chrome.
  2. 2Wait for the application to load completely.
  3. 3If an Install App or Add to Home Screen banner appears, tap it.
  4. 4If no banner appears, open Chrome's three-dot menu and look for Install app or Add to Home screen.
  5. 5Confirm the prompt and wait for the installation to finish.
  6. 6Find the LibHub icon on your home screen or app drawer and launch it.

Installation Troubleshooting

Why is the Install option missing in my browser?

The install prompt depends on your browser and connection. Ensure you open the official LibHub link in an updated version of Google Chrome (Android) or Apple Safari (iOS). Allow the page to load completely so the service worker can register. If the prompt does not appear, use the browser's menu to select "Add to Home Screen".

Why does "Add to Home Screen" not appear on iPhone / iPad?

On iOS, the option is located inside Safari's Share menu (the square icon with an upward arrow). Make sure you are using Safari, not Chrome or embedded in-app browsers like Facebook or Messenger, which do not support Safari PWA installation.

Can I use LibHub without installing it?

Yes! LibHub functions seamlessly as a responsive web application in any modern mobile browser. However, installing it to your home screen provides quick access, offline asset caching, and a full-screen native mobile experience.

What should I do if the app shows outdated catalog data?

Perform a quick refresh by pulling down the screen or clearing your browser cache. LibHub automatically caches core bibliographic data and syncs with the central database whenever an internet connection is detected.

What should I do if my digital QR pass won't scan at the counter?

Increase your screen brightness to maximum and ensure your phone display is free from severe cracks or glare. If optical scanning fails, circulation staff can quickly look up your account using your student number.

What should I do if installation fails?

Restart your browser and check your internet connection. If the issue persists, simply bookmark the web app link and access LibHub directly through your browser. You can also contact the library staff at the circulation counter for assistance.

Installability depends on your browser's Progressive Web App support and service worker registration. Installation occurs directly from the actual LibHub web application.

CDM LibHub is the integrated multi-platform library management system of Colegio de Montalban. It combines a student-facing mobile progressive web app (PWA) with a librarian desktop circulation terminal to automate cataloging, book reservations, entrance logging, and loan tracking.

CDM LibHub functions as the dedicated library service ecosystem for Colegio de Montalban. CDM: OneServe (student.cdmconnect.online) is the institutional mother system that manages college-wide student enrollment, grading, and campus services. Both systems work harmoniously to provide a unified digital campus experience.
